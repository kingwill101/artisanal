# Escapes and Entities

Escaped markers: \> not a quote, \* not emphasis, \# not a heading, and
\[not a link\].

Entities: &gt; &amp; &lt; &quot; and &#x2605; should remain intentional text.

Literal backslashes: `C:\\work\\file` and a trailing slash \\ before words.
